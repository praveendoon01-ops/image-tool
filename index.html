About Us

Welcome to our Online Image Resizer & Compressor Tool. We provide a fast, privacy-focused, and free browser-based solution for optimizing your digital images.

Our Mission

Many online forms, job applications, and portal registrations require images to be below strict file sizes (such as 50KB, 100KB, or 200KB). Our mission is to make image compression simple and accessible to everyone without requiring software installations or compromising user privacy.

Client-Side Security

Unlike standard web converters, our tool uses client-side HTML5 Canvas technology. This means your images are processed entirely inside your web browser and are never uploaded to any external server.
