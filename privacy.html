Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how our Online Image Resizer & Compressor collects, uses, and safeguards your information.

1. Image Data Security

We do not collect, store, or transmit your images. All image resizing and compression operations take place locally within your device's browser using HTML5 Canvas technology. Your files never touch our servers.

We may use third-party advertising partners (such as Google AdSense) to show ads when you visit our website. These companies may use cookies to serve ads based on prior visits to our website or other websites on the internet.

3. Log Files

Like most standard web servers, anonymous log information (such as browser type, operating system, and timestamp) may be automatically recorded by our web host provider solely for standard site management and maintenance.

4. Consent

By using our website, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its terms.
